Regola d'oro della posizione:
1. PRIMA del verbo principale → I always brush my teeth. / She never drinks coffee.
2. DOPO il verbo to be (am / is / are) → We are never late! / He is usually happy.

📖 Tappa 2: Reading INVALSI — "Leo's Busy Day"

Leggi il testo e completa le prove A, B e C in formato INVALSI.

Hi! My name is Leo and I am ten years old. I live in Bristol with my parents and my little sister, Mia.

From Monday to Friday, I always wake up at seven o'clock. I get up, wash my face and have breakfast with my family. I usually eat cereal and drink orange juice, because I never drink coffee! At eight o'clock, I go to school by bus with my best friend, Tom. We are never late.

School finishes at three o'clock. When I get home, I always have a snack and do my homework in my bedroom. On Tuesday and Thursday, I often play basketball in the park. When it rains, I stay at home and sometimes play video games or watch TV.

In the evening, we have dinner at half past seven. My dad usually cooks pasta or chicken. After dinner, I brush my teeth and I often read a comic book in bed. I go to sleep at nine o'clock... well, on Saturday I sometimes go to bed at ten!

Parte A — Multiple Choice (Scegli A, B, C o D)

0. How old is Leo? (Esempio) → C. 10 ✅

Parte B — True or False? (T = Vero · F = Falso)

0. Leo lives with his parents and his brother. (Esempio) → F (False) ✅

Parte C — Short Answers (Massimo 4 parole)

0. Who is Mia? → Leo's little sister. ✅

🎧 Tappa 3: Listening INVALSI — "Emma's Week"

Ascolta Emma parlare della sua settimana (2 volte, come alle prove INVALSI) e spunta la casella giusta.

SCRIPT PER L'INSEGNANTE (leggere due volte):

"Hello everyone, I'm Emma! Let me tell you about my week. Every morning I always brush my teeth after breakfast — that's super important! After school, I am usually very busy. I often listen to music in my room while I draw pictures. What about sports? Well, I sometimes ride my bike in the afternoon with my cousin, especially on Wednesday. And what do I never do? I never tidy my room on Monday because I have too much homework... sorry, Mum! Oh, and on Sunday morning I always make pancakes with my dad!"
